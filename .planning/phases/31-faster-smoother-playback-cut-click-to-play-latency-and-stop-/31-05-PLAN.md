<objective>
Pre-warm the resolve on exactly two user-gesture triggers — the top search result when results render, and track-menu / long-press open (D-03).

Purpose: the win on click-to-play latency comes from doing the resolve BEFORE the tap, not from failing over sooner. `ensureTrackDetails`'s readiness guard means a pre-warmed track's `play()` short-circuits with zero network work.
Output: one small pure service plus two `$effect` wirings.

**Scope guards, all locked.** `RESOLVE_WATCHDOG_MS` stays 6000 — do NOT retune any timeout (D-01). No hedged or parallel source racing (D-02). The optimistic now-playing swap is untouched; do not gate it on the `playing` event, that was tried, froze mobile playback, and was reverted (D-04). Scroll-into-view pre-warming is explicitly OUT — it is the shape that caused the `api-fetch-flood-freeze` class of bug and is a deferred idea, not a stretch goal. Spending one or two extra calls to make a play feel instant is an accepted trade (D-05).
</objective>
