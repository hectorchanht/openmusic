<objective>
Stop the queue from silently eating tracks: retry a failed next track on another source before killing it, forgive strikes when the network comes back, and surface every visible skip (D-15/D-16/D-18) — without loosening any of the guards that bound the three known freeze classes (D-17).

Purpose: the actual user complaint is a track that vanishes from the queue with no explanation, and a tunnel that blacklists half the session.
Output: a policy edit inside the existing strike/skip machinery. No new subsystem, no new timer map, no new source walk.
</objective>
