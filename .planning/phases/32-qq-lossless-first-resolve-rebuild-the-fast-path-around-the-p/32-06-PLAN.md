Add the ONE guard 32-D-15 asks for: read `Content-Length` off the response head inside
`prebufferNext` and skip the blob (stream instead) above a 24 MB ceiling, so a low-end phone never
holds a FLAC-sized Blob per advance. The ceiling is derived from CORRECTED measurements
(VALIDATION gate #7): FLAC is 959–1647 kbps ≈ 12 MB/min (a 52.8 MB song) — NOT the 7 MB/min the
original note assumed — so 24 MB admits every hq/standard tier and rejects every sq/pq FLAC.

Purpose: preserves both halves of the phase goal — "next song plays" (prebuffer kept where
affordable) and "plays fast" (no 50 MB downloads competing with the ~6-connection pool).
Output: a pure exported predicate (the testable seam — prebufferNext is private) + the guard.
</objective>
