## The Deezer tier closes kn4's residual gap

kn4 shipped with a known hole: it issued **iTunes only**, so a song iTunes does not carry produced no
carrier at all and the share card fell to the branded OpenMusic fallback. `resolveShareCover` adds a
Deezer tier behind iTunes. `deezerSongCover` returns `album.cover_*` only
(`/images/cover/<32hex>/…`; artist pictures live on a separate field), so its output always satisfies
`coverToken`'s `DZ_COVER_PATH` grammar. That is pinned by test 2, which asserts
`coverToken(url) === 'd:fe1082c5ef54876802146897e76b592e'`. Deezer's art-less placeholder has an empty
md5, fails the grammar, and simply yields no carrier — advisory, falls through to today's server chain.

## The memo moved

kn4's session memo lived in TrackMenu's `<script module>` block. It now lives in `cover-backfill.ts`
as `shareCoverMemo`, beside the chain it memoises: a service module is the natural cross-instance
home (5-6 list pages each mount their own `TrackMenu`), and every future share surface gets it for
free instead of re-declaring a second Map. It remains **hits-only** — a miss or abort is never
memoised, and an empty uid is never memoised at all (mirrors the siblings' empty-uid posture so
unrelated stubs cannot share one slot). One behavioural nuance, commented in place: a memo hit now
lands one microtask later instead of synchronously; `doShare` reads `shareCoverFallback` on a later
user tap, so the ordering is unaffected.
