<objective>
Refine the Phase-24 SONG share surface so the link is short and readable, carries only the
current song (no up-next queue), is resolvable by name+artist alone, and always unfurls an OG
card showing song name + artist.

Purpose: The shipped /song/{slug}-{source}{id}?play=<base64> link is long, opaque, and only
shows song+artist when the token is present. User wants a short /song/{slug}?n=&a= link with a
server-rendered OG card that is always populated.

Output: A new songShareUrl(track) builder, a query-param-driven SSR OG load, a client
resolve-and-play song page, and the TrackMenu share wired to the new short URL. Album/artist
share paths and the queue-restore decode path are untouched.
</objective>
