<objective>
Three download-reliability fixes found during the quick-260930-vjp album E2E:

A. Netease downloads truncate at exactly 8.00 s because the catch-all proxy's `AbortSignal.timeout(8000)` covers the whole streamed body. Make the deadline head-only for media bodies.
B. Replace the fixed 3.5 s resolve spacing in the album pipeline with adaptive handling: recognise tang's `请求过于频繁` as a RATE-LIMITED outcome in the qq adapter, fall through to another non-ytmusic source at the same download tier, and only then back off and retry qq.
C. A ytmusic song's Download label (TrackMenu main row) must describe the donor file the download will actually use, via the same lookup `downloadTrack` uses, or be absent when no donor exists.

Purpose: album "Download all" currently loses every netease-resolved song (truncated) and every tang-limited qq song ('no-audio'), and the 3.5 s pacing slows an unlimited album for nothing.
Output: head-only media deadline in the proxy; `QqRateLimitedError` in qq.ts; `'rate-limited'` DownloadResult + shared donor helpers in download-track.ts; adaptive album pipeline; TrackMenu label from the donor.
</objective>
