<objective>
Make the `/api/[source]/[...path]` catch-all answer `HEAD` without streaming the audio body.

Purpose: `download-probe.ts` `measure()` sends `HEAD` first to same-origin media URLs (`/api/netease/url?id=…`). The route has no `HEAD` export, so SvelteKit runs `GET` for it, which `fetchWithHeadDeadline`s the upstream and starts streaming the whole audio before any header leaves. The probe's 8 s `PROBE_TIMEOUT_MS` aborts it, then the `Range` fallback succeeds — ~8 s wasted on every NetEase donor lookup (YTM substitute, QQ rate-limit fallback, "Download from…" rows). Deferred item 1 of quick-260930-x3q.

Output: an explicit `HEAD` export backed by a small `upstreamHead()` helper in `$lib/proxy/http.ts` (verb-only exports rule for `+server.ts`), route tests, and a curl verification on the dev server.
</objective>
