<objective>
Skip lazyCover's `new Image()` probe for WARM+FRESH cover-cache hits. Optimization backlog item #8 (now unblocked — #2 landed the `{u,t}` write-timestamp shape).

Purpose: On a cache hit, `resolveCoverForRow` currently ALWAYS constructs `new Image()` to probe the cached URL before painting (the quick-260630-ey2 self-heal). That is one extra image load per warm visible row even when the entry was written moments ago and is overwhelmingly still live. Now that each entry carries a write-time `t` (quick-260704-2xq), a recently-written hit can be trusted and painted immediately — saving one image load per warm row on scroll.

Output: A pure freshness reader in cover-cache.ts, a `FRESH_MS`-gated fast path in lazyCover.ts, and tests. The self-heal is fully preserved for every entry whose freshness is NOT confirmed (null age / legacy / expired / >= FRESH_MS).
</objective>
