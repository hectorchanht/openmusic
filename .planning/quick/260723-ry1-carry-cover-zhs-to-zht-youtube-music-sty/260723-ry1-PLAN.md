# Quick Task 260723-ry1 — Album/artist share cards (match the song card)

Extend the quick-260723-r4p YouTube-Music-style share card to the ALBUM and ARTIST surfaces:
1. **Cover** — carry the resolved cover so `og:image` shows the art (not `/og.svg`).
2. **zhs→zht** — show Traditional name/artist on the card when the sharer's lang setting is Traditional.
3. **Simplified title + description** — `Album • Artist` / `Artist` title, `Listen on openmusic` tagline.

## Key difference from the song card (resolution safety)

The album/artist link's AUTHORITATIVE round-trip key is the **literal name in the path** (`/album/{name}?artist=`,
`/artist/{name}`) — the page resolves the tracklist by `params.name`/`?artist=` via `getAlbumTracklist`/
`enrichAlbum`/`searchAll`. The song card could safely convert its `n`/`a` (they double as best-effort
resolution carriers), but converting the album/artist PATH would make a Traditional-locale sharer's link
resolve against a Traditional query — degrading the CJK-catalog tracklist for recipients.

**So: keep the path key ORIGINAL (resolution-safe) and add converted DISPLAY carriers** (`dn`/`da`) that
the loader prefers for the OG card ONLY. The cover rides a `c` carrier (https-gated, same as the song card).

## Grounding

- Album share: `shareAlbum()` in `album/[name]/+page.svelte` → `/album/{name}?artist=` (custom, CJK-safe).
  Cover at share time = `heroImg` (`merged.cover`). Loader: `album/[name]/+page.ts`.
- Artist share: `shareArtist()` in `artist/[name]/+page.svelte` → `/artist/{name}`. Cover = `heroImg`
  (`merged.image ?? hero`). Loader: `artist/[name]/+page.ts`.
- s2t: `s2tConvertLines` + `isChineseLine` (offline, never-throw); gate on
  `effectiveTarget(settings.titleLang)` (album/artist NAME) and `effectiveTarget(settings.artistLang)`
  (album artist), resolving `'zh-Hant'` — matches the song card + `names.dn*`.
- `buildOg`/`isHttpsUrl` already https-gate the cover; og:image is emitted, never fetched → no SSRF.
